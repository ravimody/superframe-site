SuperFrame Privacy Policy

SuperFrame does not collect any data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and no servers of its own.

What SuperFrame looks at

With your permission, SuperFrame reads the photos and videos in your Photos library to group them into Chapters and suggest picks. It uses when and where they were taken and what they show, such as focus, light, faces and expressions. It does not identify who anyone is. This all happens on your iPhone or iPad.

What stays on your device

Your picks and SuperFrame’s working files are stored on your device. Like other app data, they may be included in your own device backups.

What uses the internet

SuperFrame does not upload your photos, your videos or anything about them. These Apple services may use the internet while you use the app:

  • Photos may download your own iCloud originals when SuperFrame opens them, as the Photos app does.
  • The Make Chapter screen shows an Apple Maps map of the places your photos were taken. Apple Maps loads the map from Apple.
  • If iOS asks you to rate SuperFrame, your rating goes to Apple.

When you share picks, they go where you send them.

What SuperFrame changes

SuperFrame never deletes or edits your photos. It changes Photos only when you ask: saving picks to an album, adding to or removing from an album, marking favorites, or saving a video frame as a new photo. After you save a Chapter to an album, the album keeps up with your picks until you choose Stop Syncing.
